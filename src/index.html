Quick guide

Build your first layout

Choose a board, then place a laser, mirror and detector. Press Beam, click the source, mirror and detector in order, then press Esc. Use Properties to adjust components and beam appearance. Open the example project from the public launch page to see a complete layout.

  1. Place a board. Choose the demo breadboard or pick a size.
  2. Add an assembly. Choose Flat_Mirror_frame, then click a hole. You can also drag the library card onto the board.
  3. Arrange. Drag a component, or type X and Z coordinates. Turn snapping off for free placement.
  4. Rotate. Select Rotate and drag around the mounting point, or enter an exact angle. Hold Shift for 15° steps.
  5. Keep your work. Save project downloads a reusable JSON file. Open restores it, including names and rotations.
